Step — press Step; active on load
Each wheel detent moves the Axis-dial axis by the right-dial value (0.001–10) in the active unit: inches under G20, millimetres under G21, so 0.1 is 2.54 mm under G20. Moves at the axis's full $110–$112 rate; Feed % does not slow it. 5 and 10 need 5/10 steps in Pendant settings. Waits for a known G20/G21 mode.
Continuous — press Continuous
Jogs while the wheel turns, at the right-dial 2–100% of the axis maximum rate. A 180 ms pause in wheel reports cancels the jog. Same physical speed under G20 or G21.
MPG — turn the right dial to Lead, then back to a % position
Each detent changes program feed override by 1% (10–200%). Never moves an axis. Press Step or Continuous to leave.

Lead is a right-dial position, not a jog mode: each detent changes spindle override by 1% and never moves an axis. Leaving Lead selects MPG.

In MPG and Continuous, turning the right dial also sets feed override to its percentage (2% and 5% become GRBL's 10% minimum). Axis Off or A/B/C never jogs. Fn+Continuous and Fn+Step toggle coolant instead of changing mode.

Pendant settings

Step mode only. Right-dial 5 and 10 move 5 or 10 of the active unit per detent at the axis's full $110–$112 rate: 5/10 mm under G21, 127/254 mm under G20. Continuous, MPG, and Lead ignore this setting. Selector 1 is always allowed: 1 in (25.4 mm) per detent under G20.

Changes save automatically. Safe-Z, W-Home, and Probe-Z use the shared CNCjs Tool widget settings, not pendant settings.

Default button reference

Read-only built-in behavior. Hold Fn first, then the other key.

Reset
Press: GRBL soft reset; drops pending overrides and pendant jogs.
Fn: Fn+Reset macro (no printed number).
Stop
Press: cancels jogs and queued commands; holds a running program, then resets after motion stops.
Fn: Fn+Stop macro.
Start/Pause
Press: starts the loaded CNCjs program when idle, pauses it while running, resumes it when paused, or cycle-starts a GRBL Hold.
Fn: Fn+Start/Pause macro.
Feed+ / Feed-
Press: feed override +1% / −1% (10–200%), also during a program.
Fn: Macro-1 / Macro-2.

Press: spindle override +1% / −1% (10–200%), also during a program.
Fn (printed Macro-3 / Macro-4): programmed S speed ± Spindle increment, limited by $30/$31; idle only.
M-Home
Press: GRBL homing ($H); idle or Alarm only.
Fn: Macro-5.
Safe-Z

Press: same machine-Z move as Safe-Z, then G21 G54 G0 G90 X0 Y0; no final Z move.

Fn
Modifier only.

An unassigned macro combination only logs that it is not configured; a Disabled one logs that it is disabled. Macros, homing, spindle, and coolant commands need an idle controller with no running program. Safe-Z, W-Home, and Probe-Z select G21 and leave the controller in millimetres; W-Home also leaves G54 active, and Probe-Z always sets the G54 Z offset.

Each press reads the server's current Tool settings. Unset values default to 0, matching CNCjs; 0 is not a safety guarantee. Verify the tool-change machine Z against your machine and workholding. Invalid values or a failed read send no motion. Stop, another key, disconnect, or a controller state change cancels a pending read.
